BGX2 GAMES / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy applies to the games BGX2 Games publishes on Google Play (Wheels & Deals: City Tycoon, Block Fever: Blast Puzzle, Cyber Striker, orbitHue, A Solid 10/10: Number Puzzle), to our web games played in the browser and to this website.

Information we collect

Our games have no account sign-up; in our games we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number. Game progress and settings are stored only on your device. The website has an optional email list (see “Email list” below).

Ads and third-party services

Some of our games show ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and Google Play services may collect information such as the advertising ID, device and app information, approximate location (from the IP address) and interaction data to serve and measure ads and to prevent abuse. This data is subject to Google’s policies: Google Privacy Policy and Google partner sites.

You can turn off personalised ads or reset your advertising ID in your Android settings (Google > Ads). Users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom are asked for the required consent in the game through Google’s consent form.

Web games

Nine: Kapanlar, played in the browser, uses no account, cookies or analytics and sends no data to a server. It keeps the levels you have reached in the browser’s local storage (localStorage) and remembers that the story scene was shown in session storage (sessionStorage) while the tab stays open. Adressiz Postacı also uses no account, cookies or analytics and sends no data to a server; it only keeps your music/sound settings in local storage and loads its own game files (3D models, images, sounds) from this site. Zero Day Republic: Ash also uses no account, cookies or analytics, sends no data to a server and stores nothing in the browser; it loads its game files (images, fonts) only from this site. Our web games currently show no ads; for ads see “Ads on the website and web games” below.

Website

This website currently uses no cookies or analytics. Our hosting provider GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) may keep standard server logs (IP address, browser information) for security and operation.

Email list

With the sign-up form you can give us your email address and, optionally, a nickname. The form also records the page language and the page the form was on. This information is stored in Google Forms (Google LLC) and is used only to send BGX2 game news (new games and important updates); it is not sold or shared with others. To have your entry deleted or to leave the list, write to info@bgx2games.com.tr.

On-device player profile

The nickname you choose on the sign-up page (player profile) is stored only on this device, in the browser’s local storage (localStorage, key bgx2.profile), and is used to greet you at the top of the site. It is never sent to a server and is not linked to the email list. You can remove it with the “Delete” button on the sign-up page or by clearing your browser data.

Ads on the website and web games

This website and our web games currently show no ads. When ads are enabled, Google AdSense will be used; Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies and collect information to serve and measure ads. Details: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. This policy will be updated at that time.

Children

Our games do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe such data has been collected, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights and contact

For your rights under KVKK and GDPR (access, correction, deletion, objection) or any question: info@bgx2games.com.tr. If this policy changes, the current version is published on this page.

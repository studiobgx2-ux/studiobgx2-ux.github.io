ADRESSIZ POSTACI

No addresses, only clues: get 10 letters to the right door · The Addressless Postman

About the game

The village has no street names and no door numbers; the letters only carry clues. Pick up letters at the post office, explore the 3D village on foot or by scooter and work out which house each one belongs to. Clues come as observations (flower pots, door and chimney colours, pets), locations (which side of the river, the first house after a bridge), job riddles, “not this one” eliminations, the sender’s sketch of the house and half-smudged names. Each round generates 10 new letters. A correct delivery earns coins; a wrong door costs a heart, and when all three hearts are gone the letter goes back to the post office. Stuck? Spend coins on a hint and the circle on the minimap narrows. Every villager you deliver to is written into your notebook, and the round ends with stars based on how many mistakes you made.

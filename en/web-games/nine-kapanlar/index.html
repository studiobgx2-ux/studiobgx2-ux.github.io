NINE: KAPANLAR

50 troll levels, 10 puppet bosses · Granny Traps

About the game

A puppet stole Granny’s false teeth and her pension card on payday, so she grabs her cane and shuffles after him. Get through 50 handcrafted troll levels and 10 puppet boss fights full of spike traps, swinging pendulums, bear traps, runaway wheelchairs and chattering dentures, across 10 zones from a rusty basement to the puppet’s own studio. Nothing is what it looks like: the floor lies, the flag lies, and the ceiling definitely lies. Checkpoints, zone music, English and Turkish. Die, laugh, learn the trick and try again.

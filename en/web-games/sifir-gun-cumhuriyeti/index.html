ZERO DAY REPUBLIC: ASH

Survive the Wall’s flying hound drones in a half-sunken Istanbul · Sıfır Gün Cumhuriyeti: Kül

About the game

A dark sci-fi action RPG with an isometric camera, adapted from the author’s own novel. In the half-sunken Scrap Harbor of Istanbul, Deniz tries to survive while dodging the Wall’s flying hound drones. The game is in early access: Chapter 1 is complete, Chapters 2–4 are playable and development continues. Play it in the browser in landscape, on desktop or on your phone. The game text is in Turkish.
